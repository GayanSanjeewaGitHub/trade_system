Trading Platform Policies

ACCOUNT POLICIES

1. Account Opening
- Must be 18 years or older
- Valid government-issued ID required
- SSN or EIN required for US residents
- Proof of address required

2. Account Security
- Two-factor authentication recommended
- Password must be at least 12 characters
- Regular security audits performed
- Suspicious activity monitored 24/7

3. Trading Policies
- Trading hours: 9:30 AM - 4:00 PM ET (regular market hours)
- Extended hours: 4:00 AM - 9:30 AM ET and 4:00 PM - 8:00 PM ET
- Pattern day trader rule applies (4 or more day trades in 5 business days requires $25,000 minimum)
- Good faith violations result in trading restrictions

RISK DISCLOSURE

Trading stocks involves risk of loss. Past performance does not guarantee future results. You should carefully consider your financial situation and risk tolerance before trading.

Margin Trading Risks:
- Can lose more than initial investment
- Interest charges apply to borrowed funds
- Margin calls may require immediate deposit
- Forced liquidation possible if maintenance requirements not met

PRIVACY POLICY

We collect and use your information to:
- Process trades and maintain your account
- Comply with legal requirements
- Improve our services
- Send important account notifications

We never sell your personal information to third parties.

FEES AND CHARGES

Stock Trades: 0.1% per transaction
Options Trades: $0.65 per contract
Wire Transfers: $25 outgoing, free incoming
Paper Statements: $2 per month
Account Inactivity: $10 per month after 12 months of no activity

DISPUTE RESOLUTION

All disputes must be resolved through binding arbitration. Class action lawsuits are not permitted. You have 60 days to opt out of the arbitration clause.
